CTL-1

A MIDI controller for the M-VAVE FM-1

The FM-1's keys, knobs and buttons as a MIDI controller: notes in any scale with its chords under the black keys, four banks of knobs, an arpeggiator and a Euclidean drum sequencer, over USB and Bluetooth. And General MIDI sounds of its own, so it plays with nothing connected.

Try it in the browserThe same code the FM-1 runs, with sound. Mouse, touch or keyboard; in Chrome or Edge it can play your MIDI ports. Install v1.0From Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB. Nothing to install on the computer. ManualGetting started in a few minutes, then everything else. Built on fm1-base and felucca-bsp, from Felucca by Leo Kuroshita, Hügelton Instruments.

What it does

  • Keys. 27 keys, ±3 octaves. With a scale (nine of them, any root) the white keys play its notes and the black keys its chords. Sustain on ENV, tap it twice to lock; the mod wheel on LFO. Velocity and aftertouch from the keys themselves, with no change to the hardware.
  • Knobs. Four controllers in four banks, 16 in all, each with its own number; MASTER can send one too.
  • Arpeggiator. Up, down, up-down, random or as played, 1/4 to 1/32, up to three octaves, with latch.
  • Sequencer. Four drum tracks of Euclidean rhythms, edited on the keys, on their own channel.
  • Clock. Its own, sent as MIDI clock with start and stop, or following yours.
  • Sounds. The 128 General MIDI sounds and the drum kit (Sonivox EAS), through the FM-1's speaker and headphones: PRESETS picks one.

MIDI out and in over USB and Bluetooth, and the TRS jack's MIDI in passed on.
