CTL-1

A MIDI controller for the M-VAVE FM-1

CTL-1 turns your FM-1's keys, knobs and buttons into a MIDI controller. Play notes in any scale, with its chords under the black keys. Send controllers from four banks of knobs, and run an arpeggiator and a Euclidean drum sequencer, over USB and Bluetooth. It has General MIDI sounds of its own, so it plays with nothing connected.

What it does

  • Play the 27 keys across ±3 octaves. Pick a scale (nine of them, on any root), and the white keys play its notes while the black keys play its chords. Hold ENV for sustain, or tap it twice to lock it, and hold LFO for the mod wheel. The keys read velocity and aftertouch by themselves, with no change to the hardware.
  • Turn the knobs to send four controllers in each of four banks, 16 in all, each with its own number. MASTER can send one too.
  • Hold a chord for the arpeggiator. It plays up, down, up-down, at random or as played, from 1/4 to 1/32, over up to three octaves, with latch.
  • Build beats on the sequencer: four drum tracks of Euclidean rhythms on their own channel, which you can edit on the keys.
  • Run everything on its own clock and send it as MIDI clock with start and stop, or follow yours.
  • Play the 128 General MIDI sounds and the drum kit (Sonivox EAS) through the FM-1's speaker and headphones. Turn PRESETS to pick one.

MIDI goes out and comes in over USB and Bluetooth, and MIDI from the TRS jack is passed on.
