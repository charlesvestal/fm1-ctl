Playing

  • Keys play notes, F3 to G5, and the FM-1's own General MIDI sounds play them. OCT− / OCT+: an octave.
  • ENV held: sustain; tap it twice to lock it. LFO held: the mod wheel. REC: all notes off.
  • ALGORITHM MIDI channel, PRESETS program change (the sound follows), MASTER volume.
  • Velocity and aftertouch from the keys: on the Setup page, turn SELECT for Touch.

Pages

  • A button each: HOME Play, SEL Keys, ARP Arp, SEQ Sequencer, EDIT Sound, GLO Setup (tempo).
  • KNOB 1–4 set the four values at the bottom of the screen; on Play, four controllers in four banks.
  • SELECT and FX step through the banks; hold FX to set their controller numbers.

Scales, arpeggiator, sequencer

  • Keys page, KNOB 2: a scale. Then the white keys play its notes and the black keys its chords.
  • Arp page, KNOB 1: Off, On, Latch; hold notes or a chord.
  • Sequencer: four Euclidean drum tracks. The white keys toggle steps, black keys 1–4 pick a track (or SELECT), 5–8 mute. PLAY starts it.

MIDI

  • Press MIDI ports and pick a MIDI out port to play a synth or your DAW from this page (Chrome and Edge have Web MIDI). This browser remembers the ports.
  • A MIDI in port arrives as the FM-1's USB: it plays the sounds, lights the keys, and can be the clock (Setup: Ext).

Mouse and touch

  • Drag a knob up or down, or scroll over it. Shift-click (or right-click) a button or key to hold it; click it again to let go.
  • Keys: click lower for more velocity, drag down for aftertouch. A pen's own pressure works too.

Keyboard and screen reader

  • Tab moves through the FM-1's knobs, buttons and keys. Space or Enter presses the one with focus, for as long as you hold it; Shift+Enter keeps it held until you press it again.
  • A knob turns with the arrow keys, Page Up / Page Down faster. On the keys, ← → move to the next key.
  • While the FM-1 has focus, and Keyboard shortcuts is on, the letters play it like a piano: A is the lowest key, W S E D … up to ', at the keyboard velocity set below the FM-1.
  • Also: Z / X octave, Q sustain (ENV), 1 ARP, 2 FX, Space PLAY (unless you reached a button or key with Tab), Backspace REC, Esc HOME, ↑ ↓ SELECT.
  • The screen is read out as it changes, by your own screen reader or by this page's voice (eSpeak NG, GPL-3.0), in CTL-1's own words: the FM-1 screen reader, as on the FM-1 itself. Each button and key also says whether its light is on.
